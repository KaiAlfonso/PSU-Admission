COLLEGE FOREIGN STUDENT

(International Applicants)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Full-fledged citizen of a foreign country; AND
  • Undergraduate student abroad wanting to transfer/continue at PSU; OR graduate of a foreign Senior High School

Grade Requirements:

  • No grade below C+ in all subjects

Documentary Requirements:

  • Scholastic Records / Transcript of Records (showing all grades and GWA)
  • Honorable Dismissal (if college units were earned)
  • Passport Bio-page & Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR-I) Card
  • Student Visa (if applicable) & Police Clearance from country of origin
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character from previous school
  • Birth Certificate duly authenticated by Philippine Foreign Service Post (PFSP)
  • English Proficiency Certificate (IELTS-6 band score, TOEFL-min 500 paper / 173 computer) if non-English native/medium
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee (USD $17 / Php 800) from Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
